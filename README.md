1. Introduction: No Effect Without a Real Cause

The founding rule of this article is the strict principle of causality: without a real, physical cause, nothing happens. Applied to the largest crashes in the universe — galactic mergers — the rule exposes a structural weakness in the standard account. In that account, two galaxies pass, their dark-matter halos deepen the common potential well, orbital energy is dissipated by dynamical friction against an unseen component, and the galaxies fall together. Every step is computed precisely; yet the prime mover, the dark halo itself, has never been observed as matter. It is a placeholder that makes the arithmetic work.

NASA's observatories have now mapped both ends of that state's range — hyper-active cores in quasars, and, remarkably, cores that are quiet, weak, or effectively dormant. The thesis of this article is that the quiet end is not a curiosity; it is the enabling condition of catastrophic contact.
